# Background

Malaria remains one of the leading infectious diseases in sub-Saharan Africa. Vector control relies heavily on insecticide-based interventions, particularly long-lasting insecticidal nets and indoor residual spraying. However, increasing insecticide resistance in *Anopheles gambiae* s.l. threatens the effectiveness of these interventions, making continuous resistance surveillance essential. This project investigates DDT resistance and sibling species composition in mosquito populations from selected communities in Accra, Ghana.
